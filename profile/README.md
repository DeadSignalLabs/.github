# Dead Signal Labs

> **Old Tech. New Life.**

Dead Signal Labs is an independent software development studio dedicated to preserving, modernizing, and extending classic gaming hardware and software.

Our primary focus is on open-source emulation, retro gaming infrastructure, online multiplayer, and the tools needed to keep aging platforms alive for future generations.

---

## What We Do

Dead Signal Labs develops software that bridges the gap between classic games and modern hardware.

Current areas of development include:

* 🎮 Emulator development
* 🌐 Rollback netcode and online multiplayer
* 🖥️ Cross-platform game launchers and frontends
* 📡 IPv4/IPv6 networking infrastructure
* 🔧 Development tools for emulator authors
* 🕹️ Arcade hardware research and preservation
* 📚 Documentation and reverse engineering

Many of our projects begin as solutions to real-world problems encountered while maintaining arcade hardware, preserving software, or bringing classic games online.

---

## Featured Projects

### Arcadia 20XX

A modern cross-platform emulator frontend designed around simplicity, performance, and online play.

Features include:

* Libretro-based architecture
* Rollback netcode
* LAN and Internet multiplayer
* Cross-platform support
* Raspberry Pi support
* Designed for arcade cabinets and dedicated gaming hardware

---

### Networking Platform

Originally developed to support Arcadia 20XX, our networking stack has evolved into a standalone collection of improvements for deterministic multiplayer.

Current work includes:

* Linux support
* IPv6 support
* Relay server infrastructure
* Extended peer discovery
* Connection diagnostics
* Match validation
* Rollback performance improvements

---

## Our Philosophy

Classic games deserve modern infrastructure.

Many retro gaming projects stop at accurate emulation. We believe preservation also means improving accessibility, reliability, and longevity without compromising the original experience.

That means:

* Open standards whenever practical
* Cross-platform development
* Long-term maintainability
* Thorough documentation
* Contributions back to the community whenever possible
